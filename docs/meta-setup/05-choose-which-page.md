# Step 5 — Pick which Page to read 📄

The dashboard has a **dropdown** (a little menu) where you choose **which Page or
creator** to read comments from. This page explains how to use it and how to make a
Page show up in the menu.

> ⏱️ 2 minutes per Page.

---

## Use the dropdown

1. Open the dashboard: **https://digitalpritam1.github.io/youtube-comment-dashboard/**
2. **Sign in** (type your email, click the magic link in your inbox).
3. Click the **Facebook** tab (or **Instagram**).
4. A **"Page / creator"** menu appears. Click it and **pick a Page**.
5. Click **Fetch comments**. The comments for that Page load. 🎉

> 📸 On the **Instagram** tab, the menu only shows Pages that have an Instagram
> account linked to them.

---

## "My Page is not in the menu!"

The menu can only show Pages that your robot (`dashboard-bot`) is **allowed** to see.
Giving the robot a Page has one rule:

> **A Page shows up only if you *assign* it to `dashboard-bot`.**
> Just owning the Page, or adding it to your business, is **not** enough.

### How to assign a Page to the robot

1. Go to **https://business.facebook.com → Business Settings**.
2. Make sure the folder at the top is the **right portfolio** (for us: **Indian
   Farmer**).
3. **Users → System users → `dashboard-bot` → Add assets**.
4. **Facebook Pages** → tick the Page you want → **Full control** → **Assign assets**.
5. **Instagram accounts** → tick its Instagram → **Full control** → **Save**.
6. Go back to the dashboard, **hard-refresh** the page (hold **Shift** and click
   reload), and open the Facebook tab. The Page is now in the menu.

> 🧠 The dropdown is also a **checker**: whatever you see in it is exactly what the
> robot can read. If a Page is missing, it isn't assigned yet.

---

## "The Page I want is in a *different* portfolio"

If the Page lives in **another business portfolio** (a different folder), the robot
can't even see it to assign it. You first have to **share** it across. That has its
own page:

➡️ **[Step 6 — A Page in another portfolio](06-a-page-in-another-portfolio.md)**, then
come back here for the assign step.

---

## ✅ Done?

You can pick any assigned Page from the menu and read its comments. That's the whole
job!
