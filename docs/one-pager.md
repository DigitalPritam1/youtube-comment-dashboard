# Comment Dashboard — One-Pager

**🔗 Link:** https://digitalpritam1.github.io/youtube-comment-dashboard/

**What it is:** A web dashboard that pulls **comments** from **YouTube, Facebook, and
Instagram** and helps you make sense of them — in one place, in the browser.

**What it does:**
- **Fetch** every comment (and reply) from a channel, Page, or post.
- **Analyse** them with AI — sentiment, leads, complaints, questions, themes.
- **Brief** — a short written summary of what people are asking for and what to fix.
- **Save & share** reports (each gets a private link) and **auto-refresh** nightly.
- **Export** to Excel/CSV.

**Who signs in:** Approved emails only. Sign-in is a magic link (no password).

**Setup help:** Connecting Facebook/Instagram → see
[`docs/meta-setup/`](meta-setup/README.md).
